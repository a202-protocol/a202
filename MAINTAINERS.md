# Maintainers

**Status:** Informative in full.

Maintainer status is held by named individuals, not by organisations. The rules attached to the role are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) section 2.

The maintainers are reached at **hello@a202.org** for general correspondence, at **security@a202.org** for a suspected vulnerability under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and at **conduct@a202.org** for a concern under [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The last two are read by the maintainers and nobody else.

## Adding and removing maintainers

Maintainer changes are recorded here by pull request like any other change. A fuller process for granting and removing maintainer status is settled as part of the governance review described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) section 7, and stated here when that review produces it.
